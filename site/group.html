The Group

Diversified Portfolio, Single Vision.

Lynzi Group brings together Lynzi Trading, the LYNZI Simulation Platform, Lynzi Energy, Lynzi Real Estate and Edtech.

Our Approach

Lynzi is a holding structure whose businesses share one intent: to own the asset and the upside, not facilitate a transaction.

Each business in the Group is built to be held for the long term. It is the principle behind Lynzi Trading today, and the one we carry into every sector we enter next.
