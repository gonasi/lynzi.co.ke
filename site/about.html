About Us

Built in Kenya, for the world.

A Kenya-origin, global firm building ventures across resource trade and deep technology.

Our Story

From the corridor to the world.

Lynzi is a Kenya-origin, global firm. It grew out of hands-on work in East and Central Africa's resource trade, sourcing minerals for global buyers along the DRC–Tanzania corridor.

That work shaped a wider ambition: building infrastructure that markets and technology will run on for decades, not just the deals that pass through them.

Today Lynzi holds two ventures at very different scales: a resource-trade business rooted in East Africa, and a global deep-technology platform, both built on the same principle of ownership over facilitation.

Lynzi is founded by Benard Odoyo, a Nairobi-based entrepreneur and student of Financial Economics.

Our Vision

To build the platform on which the world trades and simulates reality.
