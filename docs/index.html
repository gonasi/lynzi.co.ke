Who We Are

Where resource trade meets deep technology.

Lynzi sits at the intersection of resource trade and deep technology.

We began in the physical economy: sourcing, moving and supplying the materials that East Africa's growth depends on. Today we also build in deep technology, with a venture built for a global market. Different scales, one Kenya-born firm, and one way of working: build what lasts, and own what we build.
